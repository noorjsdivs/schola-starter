With people in place, Sunrise Academy can run a normal day. This chapter
switches between three people: **Laila** (School Admin) plans the week and
marks the staff register, **Omar** (Teacher) takes the student registers and
writes a remark, and **Sara** (Parent) asks for two days off for her daughter,
which Laila approves.

## Before you start

- **Owner** profile: `owner@sunrise.example`.
- **Teacher** profile: `teacher@sunrise.example` (password set in [chapter 5](/tutorial/add-people/)).
- **Family** profile: `parent@sunrise.example`.

## Steps

### 1. Plan the week (Laila)

<Steps>

1. In the **Owner** profile, click **Timetable** under **Daily**. "Weekly
   periods for each section. Click a period to set the subject, teacher and
   room."

2. Pick **Section** **Grade 1 · A**. An empty grid appears: periods **P1** to
   **P6** down the side with their times (08:00–08:45, 08:50–09:35, 09:40–10:25,
   10:45–11:30, 11:35–12:20, 13:10–13:55), Monday to Friday across, and
   **+ Add** in every cell.

3. Click **+ Add** in **Monday · P1**. The drawer "Monday · period 1" —
   "Subject, teacher and room for this period." — has **Starts** and **Ends**
   (already 08:00 and 08:45), **Subject**, **Teacher** ("A teacher can't be in
   two sections in the same period.") and **Room**.

4. Pick **Subject** **Mathematics**, **Teacher** **Omar Farouk**, type
   **Room** `Room 101`, and click **Save period** — **"Period saved."**

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-timetable-period-form" alt="The Monday period 1 drawer: Mathematics, Omar Farouk, Room 101" path="/app/timetable?section=…" />

5. Fill five more periods the same way, all with Omar in Room 101:

   | | P1 | P2 | P3 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Monday | Mathematics | English | Science |
   | Tuesday | English | Mathematics | Science |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-timetable-week" alt="Grade 1 · A's week: Monday and Tuesday periods 1 to 3 filled in" path="/app/timetable?section=…" />

6. **See the clash check.** Switch **Section** to **Grade 1 · B**, open
   **Monday · P1**, pick **Mathematics** and **Omar Farouk**, and save. The
   toast says **"That teacher already teaches Grade 1 · A in this period."**
   and nothing is saved. Close the drawer.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-timetable-clash" alt="The clash check: That teacher already teaches Grade 1 · A in this period." path="/app/timetable?section=…" />

</Steps>

### 2. Take the registers (Omar)

<Steps>

1. In the **Teacher** profile, sign in as `teacher@sunrise.example`. Omar's
   dashboard ("Your day at Sunrise Academy.") shows **My sections 2**,
   **Students 4** and **Registers to take** — the sections he has not marked
   today — with a **Register** button for each section.

2. Click **Student attendance** in the sidebar. "Take the register for each
   section, every school day." The **Date** is today, with **Previous day** and
   **Next day** arrows. The tiles read **Sections done 0/2** and **Not marked
   4**, and each section has a card "2 students · Not taken".

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-attendance-overview" alt="The attendance overview: two sections not taken yet" path="/app/attendance" />

3. Click the **Grade 1 · A** card. The register "Grade 1 · A — 2 students"
   lists each student by roll number with **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** and
   **Excused** buttons and a **Note** box.

4. Click **Present** for Aisha Khan and **Late** for Leo Martins, and type the
   note `Bus arrived late` for Leo. Until everyone is marked, the footer warns
   "… students are not marked and won't be saved."

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-attendance-register-marked" alt="The Grade 1 · A register: Aisha present, Leo late with a note" path="/app/attendance?section=…" />

5. Click **Save attendance** — **"Attendance saved for Grade 1 · A."**

6. Click **All sections**, open **Grade 1 · B**, click **All present**, then
   **Save attendance**. The overview now reads **Sections done 2/2**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-attendance-overview-done" alt="The attendance overview with both sections taken" path="/app/attendance" />

</Steps>

:::note[Any day can be recorded]
The run behind this page was on a Saturday; Schola saved the register anyway.
It does not block weekends or holidays, so use the **Date** picker with care.
:::

### 3. Mark the staff (Laila)

<Steps>

1. In the **Owner** profile, click **Staff attendance**. "Mark teachers and
   non-teaching staff present, absent, late, or excused." The page lists
   **Teachers — 1** (Omar) and **Other staff — 2** (Priya and Ethan), with a
   **Mark everyone present** button.

2. Mark Omar **Present**, Priya **Present** and Ethan **Late**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-staff-attendance-marked" alt="Staff attendance with Omar and Priya present and Ethan late" path="/app/staff-attendance" />

3. Click **Save staff attendance** — **"Staff attendance saved."**

</Steps>

### 4. Write a remark (Omar)

<Steps>

1. In the **Teacher** profile, click **Remarks** under **Academics**.
   "Per-student notes from teachers and admins. Visibility controls who sees
   what."

2. In **Pick a student**, choose **Section** **Grade 1 · A** and **Student**
   **SUN-2026-001 · Aisha Khan**, then click **Add remark**.

3. In the **New remark** drawer set **Type** **Academic**, **Visibility**
   **Parent — also visible to the parent**, and **Note**:
   `Aisha read her first full storybook aloud to the class today. Great confidence!`

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-remark-form" alt="The New remark drawer: Academic, visible to the parent" path="/app/remarks" />

4. Click **Save remark** — **"Remark recorded."** The remark shows under
   Aisha with Omar's name and today's date.

</Steps>

The other visibilities are **Private — only staff** and **Public — visible to
the student too**. Sara will see this one in the portals chapter.

### 5. Ask for leave (Sara) and approve it (Laila)

<Steps>

1. In the **Family** profile, sign in as `parent@sunrise.example` and click
   **Leave**. The **My requests** card says "Approved days show as excused in
   attendance."

2. Click **Request leave**. The drawer says "The school's approvers see it on
   their Leave page and decide. The outcome shows here under My requests." Fill
   it in:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | For | **Aisha Khan** |
   | Type | **Family** (the others are Sick, Casual, Other) |
   | From | `11/09/2026` |
   | To | `11/10/2026` |
   | Reason | `Family wedding out of town.` |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-parent-leave-form" alt="Sara's Request leave drawer for Aisha, Family, 9 to 10 November" path="/app/leave" />

3. Click **Send request** — **"Request sent."** It shows as **Pending**.

4. In the **Owner** profile, open **Leave**. Laila sees two tabs, **Approvals**
   and **My requests**, and the filters **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**
   and **All**. The row reads "Aisha Khan · Student · asked by Sara Khan",
   "Nov 9, 2026 – Nov 10, 2026 · 2 school days · Family", with **Approve** and
   **Decline**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-leave-approvals" alt="The Leave approvals list with Aisha's pending request" path="/app/leave" />

5. Click **Approve**. The box "Approve leave — Aisha Khan · Nov 9, 2026 – Nov
   10, 2026" has **Note (optional)** ("The person who asked sees this."). Type
   `Enjoy the wedding — Aisha is excused both days.` and click **Approve**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-leave-approve-dialog" alt="The Approve leave box with a note for Sara" path="/app/leave" />

6. The toast says **"Approved. 2 days marked excused."** Schola writes an
   **Excused** attendance mark for Aisha on both days, with the note "Approved
   leave (family)". The request moves to the **Approved** filter.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-leave-approved" alt="The Approved filter with Aisha's leave request" path="/app/leave?status=approved" />

</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

- **"That teacher already teaches … in this period."** — the clash check; pick
  another period or another teacher.
- **A student is missing from the register** — they have no active enrollment
  in that section ([chapter 5](/tutorial/add-people/)).
- **Omar cannot see a section** — check **Leads** on the Teachers page; in
  this tutorial he is the class teacher of Grade 1 and the section teacher of
  both sections.
- **Sara's "For" list is empty** — she is not linked to a child on the Parents
  page.
