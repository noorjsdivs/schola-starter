The last chapter steps back. **Reports** turn the day's records into the
numbers a head teacher asks for: who is missing school, who owes money, how
each class did. The **audit log** answers a different question — *who changed
what, and when* — for every write anyone made, including the exports you are
about to download. Finally, you remove Sunrise Academy so the database is
ready for the next recording.

## Steps

### 1. Read the reports

<Steps>

1. In the **Owner** profile, click **Reports** under **Overview**.
   "Attendance, fees and results across the school." The header has
   **Attendance CSV** and **Fees CSV**; below them **From** and **To** dates
   ("Attendance uses this range. Fees show everything overdue today."), which
   start at the first of the month and today.

2. **Attendance** — **Attendance by class** draws Grade 1's "Present + late as
   a share of recorded days." **Chronic absentees** lists "Students below 75%
   attendance with at least 5 recorded days." — for Sunrise, "No chronic
   absentees".

3. **Fees** — **Total overdue** and **Students with overdue fees** are $0.00
   and 0, and **Fee defaulters** ("Unpaid balances past their due date, largest
   first.") says "Nothing overdue": November's invoices are not due until the
   15th.

4. **Results** — pick **Exam** **Term 1 Midterm**. **Class results** ("Average
   of each student's overall %, and the share at or above 40%.") shows Grade 1:
   **4** students, **82%** average, **100%** pass rate, top student **Aisha
   Khan (91.7%)**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-reports" alt="The Reports page for Sunrise Academy: attendance by class, fees and class results" path="/app/reports" />

</Steps>

### 2. Export CSV

<Steps>

1. Click **Attendance CSV**. The browser downloads
   `attendance-<today>.csv` for the **From**–**To** range, one row per mark:

   ```csv
   Date,Admission,Student,Status,Note
   2026-11-07,SUN-2026-004,Noah Patel,present,
   2026-11-07,SUN-2026-003,Mia Chen,present,
   2026-11-07,SUN-2026-002,Leo Martins,late,Bus arrived late
   ```

2. Click **Fees CSV**. `fees-<today>.csv` has one row per invoice:

   ```csv
   Invoice,Admission,Student,Period,Total,Paid,Waived,Remaining,Status,Due
   INV-202611-7BB5E5,SUN-2026-002,Leo Martins,2026-11,310.00,310.00,0.00,0.00,paid,2026-11-15
   INV-202611-2D055C,SUN-2026-001,Aisha Khan,2026-11,310.00,0.00,0.00,310.00,pending,2026-11-15
   ```

   The same fee export is the **Export CSV** button on **Fees**; **Payroll**
   has **Export 2026 CSV** for payslips.

</Steps>

Each download is itself recorded: `report.attendance.export` and
`report.fees.export` appear in the audit log a moment later.

### 3. Read the school's audit log

<Steps>

1. Click **Audit log** under **Administration**. "Every change to your
   school's data. Append-only." Newest first, each entry shows the action, the
   time, and a line such as "resource: fee_payment · id b35b92 · req 04ac8f7e ·
   by accountant".

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-school-audit-log" alt="The school audit log with export, sign-in, payroll and fee entries" path="/app/audit-log" />

2. Scroll down to see the whole tutorial in reverse: `salary.pay`,
   `payroll.generate`, `salary_structure.create`, `fee.collect`,
   `fee_invoice.generate`, `fee_structure.create`,
   `grade.report_card.download`, `grade.enter`, `exam.create`,
   `leave.approve`, `leave.request`, `remark.create`, `staff_attendance.mark`,
   `attendance.mark`, `timetable.create`, `user_role.grant`,
   `enrollment.create`, `parent.create`, `student.create`, `staff.create`, `teacher.create`,
   `section.create`, `class.create`, `subject.create`,
   `academic_year.create`, `school.update`, and every `auth.login`.

3. Narrow it down with **Filters**: type `fee.collect` in **Action** (or set
   **From** and **To**). The hint under it reads 'Matches the start:
   "student." or "fee.collect"', so `fee.` would also keep every other fee
   action. One entry is left — Ethan's cash payment.

4. Click its **2 changes** button. A **Field / Before / After** table opens:
   the **invoice** before (`amountPaid: 0`, `status: "pending"`) and after
   (`amountPaid: 310`, `status: "paid"`), and the new **payment** with its
   receipt number. **Hide changes** folds it away.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-school-audit-log-detail" alt="The fee.collect entry expanded to show the invoice before and after, and the new payment" path="/app/audit-log" />

</Steps>

Database IDs in the expanded view (`studentId`, `invoiceId` …) read as
24-character ids, and dates as ISO timestamps. Entries written before this was
fixed stored ids as objects such as `{"i0":7007995,"i1":6252868,…}`; the page
reads those back as the same ids.

### 4. Read the platform audit log

<Steps>

1. In the **Platform** profile, click **Audit log** under **Activity**.
   "Every mutation across every tenant. Append-only." Entries look the same,
   but each says which school it belongs to ("school bbf7d3") or "platform".

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-platform-audit-log" alt="The platform audit log across every school" path="/platform/audit-log" />

2. Type `school.` in **Action** ('Matches the start: "school." or
   "auth.login"') to see only the platform's school decisions — Sunrise
   Academy's `school.signup` and `school.approve`. A full name such as
   `school.approve` narrows it to one action.

</Steps>

## Clean up

`sunrise.example` is one of the seed's reserved demo domains, so the demo seed's
reset removes Sunrise Academy along with the demo data. Pick one of these:

### Option A — `pnpm seed -- --reset` (recommended)

<Steps>

1. In a terminal at the repo root, run `pnpm seed -- --reset`.

2. The seed finds every account on a reserved domain (`schola.example`,
   `greenfield.example`, `riverside.example`, `lakeside.example`,
   `hilltop.example`, `sunrise.example`), including soft-deleted ones, and
   every school those accounts own. It deletes those schools, everything
   stored under them, their roles and memberships, and the accounts
   themselves, then seeds a fresh copy of the demo data. Its log says
   "Removed … demo schools and … demo accounts."

</Steps>

Audit log entries stay — they are append-only. An account on any other
domain that belonged to Sunrise Academy keeps its sign-in and only loses the
membership. Stripe test-mode sessions and payments stay in your Stripe
dashboard, and if `DATABASE_URL` (the Neon identity mirror) is set, the
identities also stay there; remove them from Neon separately.

<Aside type="danger" title="This cannot be undone">
The reset deletes for good, and it also replaces Greenfield and the other demo
schools with a fresh copy. Check `MONGODB_URI` first, and never run it against
production.
</Aside>

### Option B — record on a throwaway database

Before recording, point `MONGODB_URI` in `test.env` at a separate database
(for example the same cluster with a different database name), run
`pnpm seed`, record the tutorial, then drop that database in Atlas or Compass
and switch `MONGODB_URI` back. Nothing in your main database changes.

A gentler stop-gap is **Suspend…** on Sunrise Academy's row in
<Route path="/platform/schools" />. It keeps every record but pauses every
sign-in and session for its members, who are told "This school is suspended,
so sign-in is paused for everyone there. Contact the platform team to restore
access."

## Check your work

- **Reports** shows Grade 1's class result and the attendance chart.
- Both CSV files open in a spreadsheet with the columns above.
- After clean-up, <Route path="/platform/schools" /> no longer lists Sunrise
  Academy, and `owner@sunrise.example` gets "Invalid email or password.".

## If something goes wrong

- **A CSV link opens a page of JSON with `UNAUTHENTICATED`** — your session
  expired; sign in again and retry.
- **The audit log looks empty** — the filters are still set; clear **Action**,
  **From** and **To**.
- **The reset removed no schools** — the accounts were already deleted, or
  you are connected to another database.
