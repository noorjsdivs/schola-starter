An **exam** in Schola is an event — "Term 1 Midterm, 26–30 October, out of
100" — not a single paper. Teachers then fill the **gradebook**: one grid per
exam, section and subject. Schola works out each percentage and letter grade,
and a **report card** PDF gathers one student's results for one exam.

## Before you start

- **Owner** profile: `owner@sunrise.example` — creates the exam and downloads
  the report card.
- **Teacher** profile: `teacher@sunrise.example` — enters the marks.

## Steps

### 1. Create the exam (Laila)

<Steps>

1. In the **Owner** profile, click **Exams** under **Academics**. The list
   says "No exams yet".

2. Click **Add exam**. The **New exam** drawer — "An exam event for one
   academic year." — has these fields:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Academic year | **2026-2027 (current)** |
   | Name | `Term 1 Midterm` |
   | Start date | `10/26/2026` |
   | End date | `10/30/2026` |
   | Max marks | `100` |
   | Description | `Written papers in every core subject.` |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-exam-form" alt="The New exam drawer for Term 1 Midterm, out of 100" path="/app/exams" />

3. Click **Save** — **"Exam created."**

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-exams-list" alt="The Exams list with Term 1 Midterm" path="/app/exams" />

</Steps>

**Max marks** applies to every subject in the exam, so a "/100" exam means
every paper is marked out of 100.

### 2. Enter the marks (Omar)

<Steps>

1. In the **Teacher** profile, click **Gradebook**. "Enter marks per student
   for an exam, section, and subject. Percentage and letter grade are computed
   automatically."

2. In **Pick exam, section & subject**, choose **Exam** **Term 1 Midterm
   (/100)**, **Section** **Grade 1 · A** and **Subject** **MATH ·
   Mathematics**. The grid "Grades — 2 students · Max marks: 100" appears with
   **Student**, **Marks**, **%**, **Grade** and **Remark** columns.

3. Type the marks — the **%** and **Grade** columns fill in as you type. Add
   the remark `Excellent problem solving` for Aisha.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-gradebook-grid" alt="The gradebook grid for Grade 1 · A Mathematics: Aisha 92 (A+), Leo 78 (B)" path="/app/gradebook" />

4. Click **Save grades** — **"Grades saved."**

5. Change **Subject** (and then **Section**) and repeat until all six grids
   are saved:

   | Student | Section | Mathematics | English | Science |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Aisha Khan | A | 92 | 88 | 95 |
   | Leo Martins | A | 78 | 81 | 74 |
   | Mia Chen | B | 85 | 90 | 83 |
   | Noah Patel | B | 69 | 72 | 77 |

</Steps>

The letter grade comes from the percentage: **A+** 90 and above, **A** 80–89,
**B** 70–79, **C** 60–69, **D** 50–59, **E** 40–49, **F** below 40.

<Aside type="note" title="Who can type in the gradebook">
The grid is editable only with the <Perm name="grade:enter" /> permission
(Teacher and School Admin). Roles that can only read grades, such as the
Principal, see the same grid read-only, without **Save grades**.
</Aside>

### 3. Download the report card (Laila)

<Steps>

1. In the **Owner** profile, open **Students** and click **Aisha Khan**. Her
   profile header reads "Grade 1 · Section A · Roll 1 · SUN-2026-001".

2. Click the **Grades** tab. The **Term 1 Midterm** card ("Oct 26, 2026 – Oct
   30, 2026") shows **91.7%** and "Grade A+ · 275/300", then a row per
   subject.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-student-profile-grades" alt="Aisha Khan's Grades tab: Term 1 Midterm, 91.7%, grade A+" path="/app/students/…?tab=grades" />

3. Click **Report card** on that card. The PDF opens in a new tab — here
   `RC-9EEB43-SUN-2026-001.pdf`.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-report-card-pdf" alt="Aisha Khan's report card PDF: English 88, Mathematics 92, Science 95, total 275/300, 91.7% A+" path="/api/v1/student-records/…/report-card.pdf" />

</Steps>

The report card carries the school's name and address, a report-card number,
the student, class, section and roll number, the exam and its dates, a row per
subject (**Marks**, **Out of**, **%**, **Grade**), the total and overall grade,
and the student's attendance for the academic year.

The attendance line counts the academic year **up to today** — the attendance
tab stops at today too, so the two agree. Aisha's report card reads "Attendance this academic
year: 1 of 1 days (100.0%)": today's **Present**. The two **Excused** days the
approved leave wrote ahead of time (9 and 10 November) haven't happened yet,
so they are left out until their dates arrive.

## Check your work

- Each of the four students has three grades for Term 1 Midterm.
- The report card downloads for Aisha with a total of **275 / 300**.
- Parents and students see the same results on their own pages — you look at
  them in the [portals chapter](/tutorial/portals-tour/).

## If something goes wrong

- **"No students enrolled in this section for the exam's academic year"** — the
  exam's year and the students' enrollments must match.
- **The Report card button is missing** — the student has no grades for any
  exam yet, or you are in the **Load sample** preview, which hides it.
- **A mark above Max marks** — the API refuses the whole save
  (`marksObtained > maxMarks for student …`); marks run from 0 to the exam's
  maximum.
