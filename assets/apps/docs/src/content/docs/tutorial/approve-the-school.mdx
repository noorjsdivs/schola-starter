Sunrise Academy is waiting for a decision. In this chapter the Super Admin
reads the application and approves it. Approval is the switch that turns an
application into a working school: it creates the school's roles, makes the
owner its School Admin, switches the owner's account on and marks their email
verified.

## What you'll do

- Open the application on <Route path="/platform/approvals" /> and read its details.
- Approve it.
- Sign in as the owner and land on the new school's empty dashboard.

## Before you start

- **Platform** profile: signed in as `superadmin@schola.example`.
- **Owner** profile: signed out, ready to sign in as `owner@sunrise.example`.

<Api method="POST" path="platform/schools/:id/approve" /> refuses any school
that is not `pending_approval` ("Cannot approve a school in status …"). It
seeds the school's eight roles (School Admin, Principal, Vice Principal,
Teacher, Accountant, Student, Parent, Staff), sets `status: "active"` with
`approvedAt` and `approvedBy`, sets the owner's `isActive` and
`emailVerifiedAt`, gives the owner the `school_admin` role in this school,
emails "your school is approved" with a link to `/login`, adds the owner
notification "Your school is live", and writes a `school.approve` audit entry.

## Steps

### Review the application

<Steps>

1. In the **Platform** profile, click **Approvals** in the sidebar. The page
   reads "Schools waiting for a decision. Approve them or send them back with
   a reason." **Sunrise Academy** is at the top — newest first.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-approvals-list" alt="The Approvals list with Sunrise Academy pending at the top" path="/platform/approvals" />

2. Click **Sunrise Academy** to open its detail page. Check the three cards:
   - **Institution** — contact email `office@sunrise.example`, phone
     `+1 555 0142`, the address and "~120" expected students.
   - **Founding admin** — Laila Rahman, `owner@sunrise.example`, **Email
     verified** "Not yet", **Account active** "No".
   - **Lifecycle** — the **Applied** time; **Approved**, **Approver**,
     **Suspended** and **Rejected** are still empty.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-approvals-school-detail" alt="Sunrise Academy's detail page while pending: Institution, Founding admin and Lifecycle cards" path="/platform/schools/…" />

3. Click **All schools** (the back link) or **Approvals** in the sidebar to
   return to the list.

</Steps>

### Approve it

<Steps>

1. On the Sunrise Academy row, click the **⋯** button (**Open actions**). The
   menu shows the school's name, then **View details**, **Approve** and
   **Reject…**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-approvals-row-menu" alt="The row menu for Sunrise Academy with View details, Approve and Reject" path="/platform/approvals" />

2. Click **Approve**. A toast says **"School approved."** and Sunrise Academy
   leaves the Approvals list.

3. Open **All schools**. Sunrise Academy now shows the status **Active**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-platform-schools" alt="All schools, with Sunrise Academy active" path="/platform/schools" />

4. Open Sunrise Academy again. The badge reads **Active**, **Email verified**
   shows the approval time and **Account active** reads "Yes".

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-approved-school-detail" alt="Sunrise Academy after approval: active, with the owner verified and active" path="/platform/schools/…" />

</Steps>

:::tip[Rejecting instead]
**Reject…** opens a box for a reason ("Rejecting closes the application.
Reason will be emailed to the applicant."). Rejection sets the status to
`rejected`, soft-deletes the owner's account and emails the reason. Do not
reject Sunrise Academy — the rest of the tutorial needs it.
:::

### The owner's first sign-in

<Steps>

1. In the **Owner** profile, open <Route path="/login" />, sign in as
   `owner@sunrise.example` / `Schola@2026`.

2. You land on the school dashboard at <Route path="/app" />: **Welcome,
   Laila.** — "Fees, attendance, and people for this school." The sidebar
   says **Schola · School** and the bottom card **Laila · School Admin**.

3. Read the **Get your school running** checklist: "0 of 8 steps done. Work
   top to bottom — each step builds on the one before it." The eight steps
   are: Create the academic year, Add subjects, Create classes, Add sections
   to each class, Add teachers, Add students, Enroll students in sections,
   and Take today's attendance. Each step links to its page and ticks itself
   off as you go. The tiles below show **Students 0**, **Teachers 0**,
   **Collected this month $0** and **Fees outstanding $0**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-owner-first-login" alt="Laila's first view of the Sunrise Academy dashboard with the Get your school running checklist" path="/app" />

4. Click the bell (**Notifications**) in the top bar. The owner normally finds
   **"Your school is live"** — "Your Schola application has been approved."

</Steps>

## Check your work

- <Route path="/platform/approvals" /> no longer lists Sunrise Academy.
- The owner signs in and lands on <Route path="/app" />, not on "Your school's
  application is waiting for approval.".
- The platform **Dashboard** counts one more **Active** school, and **Recent
  signups** shows Sunrise Academy with an **Active** tag.

## If something goes wrong

<Aside type="note" title="If the approval email cannot be sent">
The "approved" email is best-effort: when the email provider fails, the API
logs it and finishes the approval anyway — the owner's notification and the
`school.approve` audit entry are still written. Laila can sign in either way;
the email only tells her she may.
</Aside>

- **"Cannot approve a school in status "active"."** — someone already approved
  it (for example in another tab). Nothing to do.
- **The owner still gets "Your school's application is waiting for
  approval."** — the approval did not go through. Check the school's status on <Route path="/platform/schools" />.
- **"Internal Server Error" on sign-in** — the API is restarting (for example
  after a code change in development). Wait a few seconds and try again.
