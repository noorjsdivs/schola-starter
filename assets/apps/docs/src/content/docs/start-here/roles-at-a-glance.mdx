A [role](/start-here/glossary/#role) is a job title with a fixed set of
[permissions](/start-here/glossary/#permission) attached. Schola has **nine
roles**: one for the platform and eight inside a school. There is also one
*state* that is not a role in the product sense: a **visitor**, someone who
has signed up but does not belong to any school yet.

<Roles list={['super_admin', 'school_admin', 'principal', 'vice_principal', 'teacher', 'accountant', 'student', 'parent', 'staff']} />

The server never checks a role's *name*. Every API route asks for a
permission key, and a role is simply the bundle of keys it grants. So "what can
a Teacher do?" really means "which keys does the `teacher` bundle contain?" —
and that is what this page lists.

| Principal | `principal` | One school | <Route path="/app" /> (school dashboard) | `principal@greenfield.example` |
| Vice Principal | `vice_principal` | One school | <Route path="/app" /> (school dashboard) | `viceprincipal@greenfield.example` |
| Teacher | `teacher` | One school | <Route path="/app" /> (personal dashboard) | `teacher@greenfield.example` |
| Accountant | `accountant` | One school | <Route path="/app" /> (school dashboard) | `accountant@greenfield.example` |
| Student | `student` | One school | <Route path="/app" /> (personal dashboard) | `student@greenfield.example` |
| Parent | `parent` | One school | <Route path="/app" /> (personal dashboard) | `parent@greenfield.example` |
| Staff | `staff` | One school | <Route path="/app" /> (personal dashboard) | `staff@greenfield.example` |
| Visitor (no school yet) | `user` in code | Platform | <Route path="/welcome" /> | none in the seed — sign up a new account |

Every demo account uses the password `Schola@2026` after `pnpm seed` (see
[Demo data](/setup/demo-data/) and [Demo accounts](/reference/demo-accounts/)).

"School dashboard" means the whole-school view with counts, today's
attendance and finance figures; it appears for any role holding
<Perm name="school:read:tenant" />. Everyone else gets a **personal
dashboard** about themselves — their classes, their child, their payslips.

## The roles one by one

### Super Admin

<Roles list={['super_admin']} note="platform scope — no school attached" />

The owner of the whole Schola platform. The Super Admin reviews school
applications and approves or rejects them, creates schools directly, suspends
and reactivates them, forces an admin transfer when a school's owner is
unavailable, manages platform users (invite, suspend, grant any role in any
school) and edits platform settings (branding, locale, email templates,
feature flags). They read the audit log of every school.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/platform" />, the platform console.
- **Key permissions:** <Perm name="school:approve" /> <Perm name="school:reject" /> <Perm name="school:suspend" /> <Perm name="school:create_direct" /> <Perm name="admin_transfer:force" /> <Perm name="user:assign_role" /> <Perm name="system_setting:write" /> <Perm name="audit_log:read:all" />
- **Does not have:** any school permission. The Super Admin works on schools, not inside them.

<DemoLogin as="Super Admin" email="superadmin@schola.example" go="/platform" />

### School Admin

<Roles list={['school_admin']} note="owner of one school" />

The owner of one school. Can do everything inside it: edit the school
profile, set up academic years, subjects, classes and sections, add and remove
students, parents, teachers and staff, mark attendance, enter grades, run
exams, manage fees and payroll, run the library, transport and hostel, send
announcements, read the school's audit log and export reports. Grants the
Principal, Vice Principal and Accountant roles on "Members & roles", and can
hand the school to another member through an
[admin transfer](/start-here/glossary/#admin-transfer).

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, the school dashboard. The sidebar shows every module.
- **Key permissions:** <Perm name="school:update:tenant" /> <Perm name="user:assign_role:tenant" /> <Perm name="student:delete" /> <Perm name="fee:waive" /> <Perm name="salary:pay" /> <Perm name="audit_log:read:tenant" />
- **Does not have:** the "own" read keys (`fee:read:own`, `salary:read:own` and so on). Those are for people reading about themselves.

<DemoLogin as="School Admin" email="admin@greenfield.example" go="/app" />

### Principal

<Roles list={['principal']} note="academic head" />

Runs the academic side. Can create and edit classes, sections and subjects,
assign class teachers, admit and edit students, edit teachers, create and
delete exams, write remarks, manage the timetable, approve leave, run
admissions and the library, send announcements, read the audit log and export
the attendance CSV. Reads every student's attendance and grades.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, the school dashboard.
- **Cannot:** touch money (no fees, no payroll), create academic years, delete students, add teachers, parents or staff, mark attendance or enter grades, change the school profile, or grant roles.

<DemoLogin as="Principal" email="principal@greenfield.example" go="/app" />

### Vice Principal

<Roles list={['vice_principal']} note="read-mostly academic access" />

A lighter Principal. Reads almost everything academic — classes, students,
teachers, parents, attendance, grades, exams, the timetable, admissions,
library, transport and hostel — and can write remarks and approve leave.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, the school dashboard.
- **Cannot:** create or edit anything structural, read staff records, send announcements or read the audit log.

<DemoLogin as="Vice Principal" email="viceprincipal@greenfield.example" go="/app" />

### Teacher

<Roles list={['teacher']} note="own classes only" />

Works with the classes and sections they teach. Takes the daily register,
enters marks, writes remarks, reads academic years, exams and the timetable,
and requests leave. Reads their own staff attendance and payslips. The `:own_classes`
permissions mean a teacher sees attendance, grades and remarks only for the
sections they teach.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, a personal dashboard. The sidebar starts with "My classes", "My attendance" and "My payslips".
- **Key permissions:** <Perm name="attendance:mark" /> <Perm name="grade:enter" /> <Perm name="remark:create" /> <Perm name="attendance:read:own_classes" /> <Perm name="salary:read:own" />

<DemoLogin as="Teacher" email="teacher@greenfield.example" go="/app/my/classes">
  Grace Mensah, class teacher of Grade 3 and section teacher of Grade 3 · A.
  The other 29 teachers sign in as `firstname.lastname@greenfield.example`.
</DemoLogin>

### Accountant

<Roles list={['accountant']} note="fees and payroll" />

Owns the money. Creates fee structures, collects payments, waives fees,
creates salary structures and pays salaries, and exports the fees and payroll
reports. Reads academic years, classes, sections and subjects (the Fees screen
needs them to bill a section) and students, teachers, parents and staff so they
can find who to bill or pay.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, the school dashboard (with the finance figures).
- **Cannot:** edit anything academic — the school setup pages are read only for them, and there is no attendance, grades or exams access.

<DemoLogin as="Accountant" email="accountant@greenfield.example" go="/app/fees" />

### Student

<Roles list={['student']} note="own record only" />

Reads their own attendance, grades (with the report card PDF), fees and
timetable, browses the library, and requests leave. Can pay their own invoices
online when Stripe is configured.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, a personal dashboard with "My attendance", "My grades" and "My fees".
- **Key permissions:** <Perm name="attendance:read:own_classes" /> <Perm name="grade:read:own_classes" /> <Perm name="fee:read:own" />

<DemoLogin as="Student" email="student@greenfield.example" go="/app/my/grades">

</DemoLogin>

### Parent

<Roles list={['parent']} note="linked children only" />

Reads the attendance, grades, remarks, fees and timetable of the children
linked to them, requests leave for a child, and can pay a child's invoices
online when Stripe is configured. Cannot open any other child.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, a personal dashboard; the sidebar shows "My children".
- **Key permissions:** <Perm name="grade:read:own_classes" /> <Perm name="fee:read:own" /> <Perm name="leave:request" />

<DemoLogin as="Parent" email="parent@greenfield.example" go="/app/children">
  A parent with two children at the school.
</DemoLogin>

### Staff

<Roles list={['staff']} note="non-teaching staff" />

Non-teaching staff — office, library, transport and so on. Reads their own
attendance and payslips, browses the library and requests leave.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/app" />, a personal dashboard with "My attendance" and "My payslips".
- **Key permissions:** <Perm name="staff_attendance:read:own" /> <Perm name="salary:read:own" /> <Perm name="leave:request" />

### Visitor (a signed-in account with no school)

<Roles list={['user']} note="not a spec role — a starting state" />

Anyone can create a personal account on <Route path="/signup" /> ("Personal
account" tab) or by signing in with Google or Facebook. Until a school gives
them a role, they are a **visitor**. In the code, this account holds the
platform-level `user` role, whose only permission is
<Perm name="notification:read:own" />.

- **Lands on:** <Route path="/welcome" />. From there they verify their email and can "Register a school" on <Route path="/onboarding/school" />, which sends an application for the Super Admin to review.
- **Becomes a member** when a role in a school is added to their account — for example when their school application is approved (they become its School Admin), or when a Super Admin grants them a school role on <Route path="/platform/users" />.

The spec lists nine roles (§7.1) and treats "visitor" as a label. The code
models it as a tenth role key, `user`, so that a brand-new account still
resolves to *some* permissions and can sign in. If an account somehow has no
role at all, signing in quietly gives it the `user` role again.

## How a person gets a role

| Role | How it is given |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | A Super Admin grants it on <Route path="/platform/users" />, or an operator runs the `grant-role` script (see [Running locally](/setup/running-locally/)). |
| School Admin | Given to the school's owner when the Super Admin approves the school, or when the Super Admin creates the school directly. Moves to someone else only through an admin transfer. |
| Principal, Vice Principal, Accountant | The School Admin grants or revokes them on <Route path="/app/members" /> ("Members & roles") — <Api method="POST" path="members/:id/roles" perm="user:assign_role:tenant" />. Only these three can be granted there. |
| Teacher | Created with the teacher record on <Route path="/app/teachers" />. Schola creates the sign-in and emails a "Set your Schola password" link. |
| Parent, Staff | Created with the parent or staff record when an email address is entered; the new sign-in is emailed the same "Set your Schola password" link. |
| Student | Not created from the student form — see the caution below. |
| Any role | A Super Admin can grant any role in any school on <Route path="/platform/users" />. |

:::caution[Gaps found in the code]
- **Students:** the student form (`createStudentSchema` in `packages/validation/src/people.ts`) has no email field, so there is no screen that gives a student a sign-in linked to their record. The demo student's link comes from the seed.
- **Existing accounts as parents:** if the email already belongs to an account, the parent record is linked to it but the `parent` role is not added, so that person still lands on `/welcome`.
:::

## One person, several roles

A person can hold more than one role in the same school — for example a
teacher who is also the accountant. Then:

- **Permissions add up.** The person gets every key from every role they hold in that school.
- **One role is primary.** The highest role decides which dashboard and console they get, in this order: Super Admin, School Admin, Principal, Vice Principal, Accountant, Teacher, Staff, Parent, Student, then the visitor `user` role.
- **The school is chosen at sign-in.** If an account belongs to more than one school, sign-in picks the first of those schools that is `active`. There is no school switcher yet. If none is active (suspended, pending or rejected), sign-in is refused.

The demo Accountant shows the "links" idea too: their account is tied to a
staff record (Ibrahim Ahmed, Senior Accountant), so "My attendance" appears in
their sidebar even though their role is Accountant. The "My …" pages appear
for whoever is linked to a student, parent, teacher or staff record, not by
role alone.
