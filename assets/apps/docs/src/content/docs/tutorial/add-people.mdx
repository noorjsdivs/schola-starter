A school is its people. In this chapter Laila fills Sunrise Academy: the
teacher she added in the last chapter sets his password, two office staff join,
four children are admitted and placed in Grade 1, one child's mother gets a
parent sign-in, and the office's accountant gets the **Accountant** role so he
can run fees and payroll.

Two ideas matter here:

- **A person record and a sign-in are different things.** Teachers always get
  a sign-in. Staff and parents get one only if you give them an email. Every
  new sign-in is emailed a link to set its own password. Students
  added on the Students page get none (see the portals chapter).
- **Admitting is not placing.** A student record says who the child is; an
  *enrollment* puts them in a section for the year, which is what puts them on
  a register, in the gradebook and on a fee run.

## What you'll do

- Set Omar Farouk's password from his set-password link.
- Add staff **Priya Das** (Office Manager) and **Ethan Brooks** (Accountant).
- Admit **Aisha Khan**, **Leo Martins**, **Mia Chen** and **Noah Patel**, and
  place them in Grade 1 · A and B with roll numbers.
- Add **Sara Khan**, Aisha's mother, with a parent sign-in.
- Give Ethan the **Accountant** role on **Members & roles**.

## Before you start

- **Owner** profile: signed in as `owner@sunrise.example`.
- Grade 1 with sections A and B exists, led by Omar ([chapter 4](/tutorial/set-up-academics/)).

## Steps

### 1. Omar sets his password

<Steps>

1. Omar opens the email **"Set your Schola password for Sunrise Academy"**. It
   says he has been added to Sunrise Academy on Schola as a teacher, names his
   sign-in email, and has a **Set password** button. The link opens
   <Route path="/reset-password" /> with a one-time token.
   With the console transport, copy the link from the API log line
   `[email/console] would have sent email`.

2. The page is titled **Set a new password** — "Choose a strong password — at
   least 8 characters, with upper, lower, and a digit." Type **New password**
   `Schola@2026`.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-teacher-set-password" alt="The Set a new password page with a Strong password typed" path="/reset-password?token=…" />

3. Click **Save new password**. The toast says **"Password updated. Please
   log in."** and you land on <Route path="/login" />. Setting the password also
   marks Omar's email as verified.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="How long the link lasts">
The email says "This link expires in 24 hours and works once." That is
`PASSWORD_RESET_TTL_MIN` × 24 minutes (60 × 24 by default) — longer than a
"Forgot password" link, because nobody asked for this one and it may sit
unread for a while. If it runs out, **Forgot?** on the sign-in page sends a
fresh link.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="No email arrived?">
In the run behind this page the email provider could not deliver to
`teacher@sunrise.example` (see [chapter 2](/tutorial/register-a-school/)), so
no link existed. Adding Omar still succeeded — the invite is best-effort — and
the run stood in for the email by creating a one-time `password_reset` token
for that test account and opening the same `/reset-password?token=…` page.
With the console transport you never need this — the link is in the API log.
</Aside>

### 7. Staff and parents set their password

<Steps>

1. In the person's own browser profile, open their email **"Set your Schola
   password for Sunrise Academy"** and click **Set password** — or, with the
   console transport, copy the link from the API log.

2. Set `Schola@2026` on **Set a new password**, as Omar did, and click **Save
   new password**.

3. Repeat for `accountant@sunrise.example` and `parent@sunrise.example`.

</Steps>

If a link has run out or never arrived, use **Forgot?** next to **Password**
on <Route path="/login" />: type the email on **Reset your password** and click
**Send reset link**. The page always answers the same way — "If that email is
registered, you'll receive a reset link shortly." — whether or not the email
could be delivered, so it never reveals which addresses have accounts. The
**"Reset your Schola password"** link is valid for `PASSWORD_RESET_TTL_MIN`
minutes (1 hour by default).

## If something goes wrong

- **"Another record already uses this admission number."** — admission numbers
  are unique in the school.
- **"A user with this email already exists."** — that email already has an
  account (teachers and staff need a new one). Use a different email, or ask a
  Super Admin to give the existing account a role.
- **A section is greyed out with "— full"** — it has reached its capacity.
- **"This is their only role here. Give them another role first, or remove
  them from People."** — you tried to remove someone's last role on Members &
  roles.
