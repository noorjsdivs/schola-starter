A new school is an empty shell. Before anyone can take a register or send an
invoice, Schola needs the school's *structure*: which
[academic year](/start-here/glossary/) is running, which subjects are taught,
and which classes and [sections](/start-here/glossary/#section) exist. Every
later chapter hangs off these records — a register belongs to a section, a
section to a class, and a class to one academic year.

## What you'll do

- Check and save the school's details on **School settings**.
- Create the academic year **2026-2027** and mark it current.
- Add the subjects **Mathematics**, **English** and **Science**.
- Add the first teacher, **Omar Farouk**, so you can pick him as the class and
  section teacher.
- Create the class **Grade 1** with sections **A** and **B**.

## Before you start

- **Owner** profile: signed in as `owner@sunrise.example` (School Admin).
- You are on the dashboard at <Route path="/app" /> with the **Get your school
  running** checklist at 0 of 8.

<Aside type="note" title="Why the teacher comes before the class">
A teacher is not required: **Class teacher (optional)** and **Section teacher
(optional)** can stay on "No class teacher" / "No section teacher", and you can
assign one later from the row menu. This chapter adds Omar first only so it can
pick him in both forms and finish the class in one pass.
</Aside>

## Steps

### School settings

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, scroll to **Administration** and click **School settings**.
   The page says "School details and admin transfer. Your own account lives in
   My profile."

2. The **School profile** card shows what Laila typed on the application:
   **School name** "Sunrise Academy", **Type** "School (K-12)", **Expected
   students** 120, **Address**, **Contact email**, **Contact phone**, and
   **Locale** `en-US` and **Currency** `USD` (the defaults).

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-school-settings" alt="School settings with the School profile card and the Transfer admin role card" path="/app/settings" />

3. Change **Expected students** to `150`. **Save changes** becomes clickable
   once something has changed. Click it — the toast says **"School profile
   saved."**

</Steps>

The currency set here is what every invoice, receipt and payslip uses
(<Api method="PATCH" path="schools/current" perm="school:update:tenant" />).
Leave **Transfer admin role** alone — it hands the school to someone else.

### The academic year

<Steps>

1. Click **Academic years** under **School setup**. The list is empty: "No
   academic years yet".

2. Click **Add academic year**. The **New academic year** drawer opens.

3. Fill it in and tick the box:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Name | `2026-2027` |
   | Start date | `08/01/2026` |
   | End date | `06/30/2027` |
   | Make this the current academic year | ticked |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-academic-year-form" alt="The New academic year drawer filled in for 2026-2027" path="/app/academic-years" />

4. Click **Create academic year**. The toast says **"Academic year
   created."** and the row shows a **current** tag.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-academic-years-list" alt="Academic years with 2026-2027 marked current" path="/app/academic-years" />

</Steps>

Only one year can be current; it is the year new classes, enrollments, fee
structures and the dashboard use by default.

### Subjects

<Steps>

1. Click **Subjects**, then **Add subject**.

2. Fill in **Name**, **Code** and **Description (optional)**, then click
   **Create subject**. Repeat for all three:

   | Name | Code | Description |
   |---|---|---|
   | `Mathematics` | `MATH` | `Numbers, shapes and problem solving` |
   | `English` | `ENG` | `Reading, writing and speaking` |
   | `Science` | `SCI` | `The natural world, through experiments` |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-subject-form" alt="The New subject drawer for Mathematics, code MATH" path="/app/subjects" />

3. Each save shows **"Subject created."** The list now has three rows.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-subjects-list" alt="The Subjects list with English, Mathematics and Science" path="/app/subjects" />

</Steps>

### The first teacher

<Steps>

1. Click **Teachers** under **People**, then **Add teacher**. The **New
   teacher** drawer says "Creates their sign-in and emails a link to set a
   password."

2. Fill it in:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Employee ID | `SUN-T001` |
   | Email | `teacher@sunrise.example` |
   | First name / Last name | `Omar` / `Farouk` |
   | Phone (optional) | `+1 555 0150` |
   | Joined (optional) | `08/01/2026` |
   | Qualification (optional) | `B.Ed Primary Education` |
   | Subjects they teach | tick **English**, **Mathematics** and **Science** |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-teacher-form" alt="The New teacher drawer for Omar Farouk with three subjects ticked" path="/app/teachers" />

3. Click **Add teacher**. The toast says **"Teacher added — they'll get an
   email to set a password."** Omar appears in the list with his subjects and
   "Not assigned" under **Leads**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-teachers-list" alt="The Teachers list with Omar Farouk" path="/app/teachers" />

</Steps>

Saving a teacher creates three things at once: Omar's sign-in account, his
**Teacher** role in Sunrise Academy, and his teacher record
(<Api method="POST" path="teachers" perm="teacher:create" />). He sets his own
password from the email; you do that in the next chapter.

<Aside type="note" title="If the set-password email can't be delivered">
The email is sent after Omar is saved, and it is best-effort. When the provider
cannot deliver it, the API still answers `201` and the toast still appears; the
API log notes the failure and the `teacher.create` audit entry records
`inviteEmail: "failed"`. Omar can then use **Forgot?** on the sign-in page, or
you can follow [chapter 5](/tutorial/add-people/) with the console transport.
</Aside>

### The class and its sections

<Steps>

1. Click **Classes & sections**, then **Add class**. The **New class** drawer
   says "A class belongs to one academic year. Sections are added inside it."

2. Fill it in:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Academic year | **2026-2027 (current)** (already picked) |
   | Name | `Grade 1` |
   | Description (optional) | `First year of primary school` |
   | Class teacher (optional) | **Omar Farouk (SUN-T001)** |
   | Subjects taught | tick **English**, **Mathematics** and **Science** |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-class-form" alt="The New class drawer for Grade 1 with Omar Farouk as class teacher" path="/app/classes" />

3. Click **Create class**. The toast says **"Class created."**

4. Click **Grade 1** in the list. The class page reads "2026-2027 · Class
   teacher: Omar Farouk", with tiles **Sections 0**, **Students 0** and
   **Seats —**, and "No sections yet".

5. Click **Add section**. In the **New section** drawer ("A group of students
   inside Grade 1.") type **Name** `A`, **Capacity** `30` ("0 means no limit.")
   and pick **Section teacher (optional)** **Omar Farouk (SUN-T001)**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-section-form" alt="The New section drawer: name A, capacity 30, section teacher Omar Farouk" path="/app/classes/…" />

6. Click **Create section** — **"Section created."** Repeat for section `B`,
   capacity `30`, section teacher **Omar Farouk**.

7. The class page now shows **Sections 2**, **Students 0**, **Seats 60**, and
   both sections at "0/30".

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-class-detail-sections" alt="Grade 1 with sections A and B, each 0/30, led by Omar Farouk" path="/app/classes/…" />

</Steps>

## Check your work

- Back on <Route path="/app" />, the checklist ticks **Create the academic
  year**, **Add subjects**, **Create classes**, **Add sections to each class**
  and **Add teachers**.
- <Route path="/app/teachers" /> shows Omar leading "Grade 1 · A" and "Grade 1 ·
  B".
- <Route path="/app/classes" /> lists Grade 1 in 2026-2027.

## If something goes wrong

- **A red message under a field** — fix that field and click **Create class**
  (or **Create section**) again. The teacher pickers may stay empty.
- **"Could not save the class."** with a field message — the name is empty or
  longer than 60 characters, or no academic year is picked.
- **Section full** later on — raise **Capacity** with the section's row menu,
  or set it to `0` for no limit.
- **You cannot delete the academic year** — "Only years without classes can be
  deleted." Delete its classes first.
