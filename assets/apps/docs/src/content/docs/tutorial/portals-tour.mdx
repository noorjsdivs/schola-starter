Sunrise Academy through the eyes of the people it serves. They all use the
same address, <Route path="/app" />, but each sees a *member dashboard* and a
sidebar cut down to their own permissions — nobody sees a menu item they cannot
use.

### 2. The student

<Aside type="note" title="Why this step uses the demo school">
A student sign-in needs a user account linked to the student record, and as of
this writing nothing in the web app creates one: the **New student** form has
no email field, **Members & roles** only adds roles to people who already
sign in, and no other screen links a student to an account. Only `pnpm seed`
does, for one demo student. So Aisha, Leo, Mia and Noah cannot sign in, and
this step uses **Adam Singh** (`student@greenfield.example`, Grade 8 · A at
Greenfield International School) to show the student portal.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. In a spare profile, sign in as `student@greenfield.example`. **Welcome,

   shows **Today** (Present), his class, **Attendance this year**, his
   **Latest exam** (Term 1 — Midterm with its grade) and **Fees due**, then

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-student-dashboard" alt="Adam Singh's student dashboard at Greenfield" path="/app" />

## If something goes wrong

- **A parent sees no children** — link the child on the Parents page (edit the
  parent, **Children at this school**).
- **A teacher's dashboard says there are no sections** — make them the class
  teacher or a section teacher on the class page.
- **Ethan (Accountant) lands on the school dashboard, not a member
  dashboard** — that is expected: roles that can read the school profile get
  the school dashboard.
