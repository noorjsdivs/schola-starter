Payroll mirrors fees, but the money goes the other way. A **salary
structure** is a pay scale — a base amount plus earnings and deductions. A
**payroll run** applies a structure to chosen teachers and staff for one month
and creates a **payslip** for each. The school then pays each payslip, either
outside Schola (bank transfer, cash, cheque) and marks it paid, or by card with
Stripe.

## Before you start

- **Accountant** profile: `accountant@sunrise.example` (needs
  <Perm name="salary_structure:create" /> and <Perm name="salary:pay" />; a
  School Admin has them too).
- The teacher and both staff members exist ([chapter 5](/tutorial/add-people/)).

## Steps

### 1. Create the salary structures

<Steps>

1. Click **Payroll** under **Finance**. "Salary structures and monthly payroll
   runs." The tabs are **Salary Structures** and **Payroll Runs**; the header
   has **Export 2026 CSV**.

2. On **Salary Structures**, click **Add structure**. The **New salary
   structure** drawer says "Base pay and components for a role."

3. Type **Name** `Teacher — Grade 1` and **Base amount** `3200`.

4. Click **Add component** twice and fill the two rows (name, amount, kind):

   | Name | Amount | Kind |
   |---|---|---|
   | `Housing allowance` | `400` | **Earning** |
   | `Income tax` | `350` | **Deduction** |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-salary-structure-form" alt="The New salary structure drawer: Teacher — Grade 1, base 3200, housing allowance and income tax" path="/app/payroll" />

5. Click **Save** — **"Salary structure created."**

6. Add a second structure the same way: **Name** `Office staff`, **Base
   amount** `2400`, components `Transport allowance` `150` **Earning** and
   `Income tax` `250` **Deduction**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-salary-structures" alt="The Salary Structures tab with Teacher — Grade 1 and Office staff" path="/app/payroll" />

</Steps>

Net pay is base + earnings − deductions: **$3,250** for the teacher scale and
**$2,300** for office staff.

### 2. Generate November's payslips

<Steps>

1. Click **Payroll Runs**, then **Generate payroll**. The **Generate payroll**
   card opens with **Year** `2026` and **Month** **November** already set.

2. Pick **Salary structure** **Teacher — Grade 1 ($3,200.00)** and, in
   **Teachers**, click **Omar Farouk (SUN-T001)**. (Both lists take several
   picks: "Hold Ctrl/Cmd to multi-select.")

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-payroll-generate-teacher" alt="Generate payroll for November 2026: Teacher — Grade 1 for Omar Farouk" path="/app/payroll" />

3. Click **Generate** — **"Generated 1 payslip(s) (0 already existed)."**

4. Click **Generate payroll** again. Pick **Office staff ($2,400.00)** and, in
   **Non-teaching staff**, select both **Ethan Brooks (SUN-S002)** and **Priya
   Das (SUN-S001)**. Click **Generate** — **"Generated 2 payslip(s) (0 already
   existed)."**

5. The table lists three payslips for **November 2026**, each **Pending**. The
   **Employee** column names whose payslip each row is — the name, then the
   employee ID and **Teacher** or **Staff** — followed by **Period**, **Net**,
   **Status**, a **Payslip** link and **Mark paid** and **Pay** buttons.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-payroll-runs" alt="Three pending November payslips with Payslip, Mark paid and Pay" path="/app/payroll" />

</Steps>

### 3. Mark the teacher's payslip paid

<Steps>

1. On Omar Farouk's row (**SUN-T001 · Teacher**, **$3,250.00** net), click
   **Mark paid**. The drawer **Mark as paid** reads "Omar Farouk ·
   PAY-202611-E77A88 · November 2026 · $3,250.00. For salary paid outside
   Schola — no card is charged."

2. Keep **Paid on** (today) and **Method** **Bank transfer** (the others are
   Cash, Cheque, Online, Other). Type **Reference (optional)** `Bank transfer
   ref. NOV-2026-001` ("Bank transaction or cheque number.") and **Notes
   (optional)** `November salary`.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-payroll-mark-paid" alt="The Mark as paid drawer for PAY-202611-E77A88, bank transfer with a reference" path="/app/payroll" />

3. Click **Mark as paid** — **"Payslip PAY-202611-E77A88 marked as paid."**
   The row's status turns **Paid** and its **Mark paid** and **Pay** buttons
   disappear.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-payroll-runs-paid" alt="The payroll runs with the teacher's payslip paid" path="/app/payroll" />

</Steps>

### 4. Download the payslip

<Steps>

1. Click **Payslip** on the paid row. The PDF (`PAY-202611-E77A88.pdf`) opens
   in a new tab.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-payslip-pdf" alt="Omar Farouk's November payslip: base 3200, housing 400, income tax 350, net 3250, paid by bank transfer" path="/api/v1/salary-records/…/payslip.pdf" />

2. Omar sees the same payslip on his own **My payslips** page, marked
   **Paid** with the date — you will look at it in the next chapter.

</Steps>

The payslip shows the school, the payslip number and month, the employee with
their ID and qualification, the status and paid date, **Earnings** (base
salary and each earning, **Gross earnings**), **Deductions** (each deduction,
**Total deductions**), **Net pay**, and how it was paid — "Payment method: Bank transfer" followed by
the reference.

:::note[Paying by card instead]
**Pay** on a pending payslip opens a Stripe Checkout session so the school pays
that payslip by card. Stripe returns to <Route path="/app/payroll" /> with
`?checkout=success`, and the webhook marks the payslip paid (`salary.pay` in
the audit log, actor role `stripe`). This tutorial uses **Mark paid** instead.
:::

## Check your work

- One payslip is **Paid** and two are **Pending** for November 2026.
- **Export 2026 CSV** downloads the year's payslips.
- Generating November again for the same people answers "(… already existed)"
  and creates nothing.

## If something goes wrong

- **Mark paid / Pay are missing** — you lack <Perm name="salary:pay" />, or the
  payslip is already paid.
- **Nobody listed under Teachers or Non-teaching staff** — add them first on
  the People pages.
- **A wrong structure was used** — payslips record the amounts at generation
  time; editing the structure later does not change them.
