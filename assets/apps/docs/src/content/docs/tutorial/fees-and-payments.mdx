Money in Schola moves in three steps. A **fee structure** says what the school
charges ("Grade 1 monthly fee: tuition, lunch, activities"). **Invoices** turn
it into a bill per student for one period. **Payments** settle the bills —
recorded at the office, or paid online by the family through Stripe Checkout.
Every payment gets a receipt PDF.

## Steps

### 1. Create the fee structure

<Steps>

1. Sign in as Ethan. His sidebar shows **Finance › Fees** and **Payroll**,
   plus his own **My attendance** and **My payslips** (he is also staff).

2. Click **Fees**. "Fee structures, invoices, and collected payments." There
   are three tabs — **Structures**, **Invoices**, **Payments** — and an
   **Export CSV** button.

3. On **Structures**, click **Add structure**. The **New fee structure** drawer
   says "A fee the school can invoice."

4. Fill it in:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Academic year | **2026-2027 (current)** |
   | Frequency | **Monthly** (or One-time, Quarterly, Yearly) |
   | Name | `Grade 1 monthly fee` |
   | Line items | `Tuition` `250`, then **Add line item** `Lunch` `40`, then **Add line item** `Activities` `20` |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-fee-structure-form" alt="The New fee structure drawer: Grade 1 monthly fee with Tuition, Lunch and Activities" path="/app/fees" />

5. Click **Save** — **"Fee structure created."** The row shows **Monthly** and
   a total of **$310.00**.

</Steps>

### 2. Generate November's invoices

<Steps>

1. Click the **Invoices** tab, then **Generate invoices**. The card
   **Generate invoices for a section** opens (the button turns into
   **Cancel**).

2. Fill it in:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Fee structure | **Grade 1 monthly fee ($310.00)** |
   | Period key | `2026-11` ("e.g. 2026-06 or 2026-Q1") |
   | Class | **Grade 1** |
   | Section | **A** |
   | Due date | `11/15/2026` |

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-fee-generate" alt="Generate invoices for a section: Grade 1 monthly fee, period 2026-11, Grade 1 A, due 15 November" path="/app/fees" />

3. Click **Generate** — **"Generated 2 invoice(s) (0 already existed)."**
   Click **Generate invoices** again and repeat for **Section** **B**.

4. The table lists four invoices. Each row shows the **Student** (name and
   admission number), the **Invoice** number such as `INV-202611-7BB5E5`, the
   **Period** `2026-11`, **Total** $310.00, **Paid** $0.00, **Status**
   **Pending**, the **Due** date, and two buttons: **Record** and **Pay**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-fee-invoices" alt="Four pending November invoices with Record and Pay buttons" path="/app/fees" />

</Steps>

Generating again for the same structure, period and section skips students who
already have that invoice, so it is safe to repeat.

### 3. Record a cash payment

<Steps>

1. Click **Record payment** above the table. (**Record** on Leo's row opens
   the same drawer with his invoice already chosen.) The drawer says "Cash,
   bank transfer or cheque received at the office. A receipt is issued."

2. In **Student**, type `Leo` in **Search students by name or number** and
   click **Leo Martins — SUN-2026-002 · Grade 1 · A**. He is pinned at the top
   with a **Change** link.

3. **Invoice** lists all of his open invoices; pick **INV-202611-7BB5E5 ·
   2026-11 · remaining $310.00**. **Amount** fills itself with the balance,
   `310`.

4. Leave **Method** on **Cash** (the others are Bank transfer, Online, Cheque,
   Other) and type **Reference (optional)** `Front office, cash`.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-fee-record-payment" alt="The Record payment drawer for Leo Martins: invoice picked, amount 310, method Cash" path="/app/fees" />

5. Click **Record payment** — **"Payment recorded."** Leo's invoice now shows
   **Paid** $310.00 and status **Paid**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-fee-invoices-after-cash" alt="The invoices after the cash payment: Leo Martins paid" path="/app/fees" />

</Steps>

You can also type a smaller amount: the invoice becomes **Partial** and the
rest stays open.

### 4. Download the receipt

<Steps>

1. Click the **Payments** tab. The row shows the **Receipt** number, when it
   was **Received**, the **Amount** and the **Method**.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-fee-payments" alt="The Payments tab with Leo's cash payment and a Download PDF link" path="/app/fees" />

2. Click **Download PDF**. The receipt (`RCT-202611-E86504.pdf` in this run)
   opens in a new tab.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-receipt-pdf" alt="The receipt PDF: payment received from Leo Martins, $310.00, balance remaining $0.00" path="/api/v1/fee-payments/…/receipt.pdf" />

</Steps>

The receipt shows the school, the receipt number and date, who paid, the
invoice, period and method with its reference, each line item, the **Invoice
total**, **This payment**, **Total paid to date** and **Balance remaining**.

### 5. Pay online as the parent

<Steps>

1. In the **Family** profile, sign in as Sara and open **My children ›
   Aisha Khan**, then the **Fees** tab. The **Invoices** table lists
   November's invoice with its **Balance** $310.00 and a **Pay online** button.

   <Screenshot name="tutorial-parent-child-fees" alt="Sara's view of Aisha's fees with the Pay online button" path="/app/children/…?tab=fees" />

2. Click **Pay online**. Schola opens a Stripe Checkout session for the
   remaining balance and the browser leaves for `checkout.stripe.com`. In test
   mode the page is marked **Sandbox** and shows "Invoice INV-202611-2D055C"
   and $310.00. Stripe may also offer the payer's local currency.

3. Type any email, the test card **4242 4242 4242 4242**, any future expiry
   such as **12 / 34**, any CVC such as **123**, a cardholder name, and click
   **Pay**.

4. Stripe sends Sara back to Aisha's page with `?checkout=success`. The toast
   says **"Payment received. The receipt appears here once Stripe confirms
   it."**

5. A moment later Stripe's `checkout.session.completed` event reaches the API
   (through `stripe listen` in development). Schola records an **Online**
   payment with the note "Paid with Stripe Checkout.", issues a receipt and
   marks the invoice **Paid**. Reload the tab to see it.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Steps 3 to 5 were not completed in this run">
The automated run behind this page reached the Stripe Checkout page (step 2) —
the checkout session was created and the browser was redirected — but stopped
there: the hosted page carries Stripe's own bot check, which a script must not
answer. Steps 3 to 5 describe what the code does
(<SourceFile path="apps/api/src/modules/stripe/stripe.webhook.ts" /> and
<SourceFile path="apps/api/src/services/stripe/fulfill.ts" />). When you record
the video, a person completes the payment normally.
</Aside>

If Sara clicks the back arrow on Stripe's page instead, she returns with
`?checkout=cancel` and the toast "Payment cancelled. Nothing was charged."

## Check your work

- The **Invoices** tab shows Leo **Paid** and the other three **Pending** (or
  Aisha **Paid** too, after a completed Stripe payment).
- The dashboard's **Collected this month** reads **$310** and **Fees
  outstanding** **$930**.
- **Export CSV** downloads `fees-<date>.csv` with one row per invoice:
  Invoice, Admission, Student, Period, Total, Paid, Waived, Remaining, Status,
  Due.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This invoice cannot be paid."** — it is already paid, waived or
  cancelled.
- **The online payment does not appear** — the webhook did not arrive. Check
  that `stripe listen` is running and that `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` matches it;
  without the secret the webhook answers `503`. Stripe retries, and fulfilment
  is idempotent on the Checkout session id.
- **The student search finds nobody** — the drawer searches name or admission
  number; try `SUN-2026-002`.
